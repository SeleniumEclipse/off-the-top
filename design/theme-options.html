What stays in every option

Your actual Set fonts. Teal as the one accent. Flat colors, hard edges, readable cards. No gradients, emoji icons, promo copy, or extra text under every control.

The physical card edges, score flaps and ticket seams are theme proposals, not a return to unrelated decoration. All artwork here is original; the references are inspiration, not copied screens.

Try it: tap a deck to see the round. Pass and Correct change sample cards. Use the top controls to compare the same screen in all three. These previews do not use your phone’s sensors or save scores.
