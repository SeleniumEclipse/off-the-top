# Off the Top 1.4.0 — Card Table

- Built the approved card-table design natively for Android.
- Flat green background with **no poker-table outline**.
- Three cream card stacks with red patterned backs.
- Original leaf, mug and motion icons in the card corners—no suits or emoji.
- **Green for correct, red for pass**, with the word, timer and score on a playing card.
- Matching results screen, night colors and app icon. Same blocky Set-style fonts and buttons.

**Download Off-the-Top-1.4.0.apk below and install over the existing app.** No uninstall needed; settings, history and played-card memory stay intact. Android 8.0+.

All three decks and 1,844 cards remain. The tilt engine is unchanged. **137 unit tests and 26 Android tests passed**, including large-text layout, exact category artwork, feedback colors and saved rounds. The signed APK was tested through the emulator's sensor stack in both landscape directions. Physical-phone feel remains subject to hands-on testing.
