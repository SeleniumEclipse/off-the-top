# Off the Top — first edition

An offline Android party game in the same warm-newsprint style as Set.

## Install

Download **Off-the-Top-1.0.0.apk** from the assets below and open it on your Android phone. If Android asks, allow installation from the browser or file manager used to open the APK. Requires **Android 8.0 or newer**. No account, internet connection, payment, ads, camera or microphone needed.

## Three complete decks — 1,844 original cards

- **Wild World — 622 cards:** animals, plants, weather, Earth and space.
- **Everyday Things — 617 cards:** objects, food, clothing, tools, instruments and transport.
- **Do Your Thing — 605 cards:** activities, jobs, sports, places and everyday situations. Great for charades too.

No pop-culture or celebrity decks. No duplicate topics within or across the three decks.

## How it plays

Choose a deck, hold the phone sideways against your forehead with the screen facing your friends, and guess their clues. Tilt the **screen toward the floor for correct**, or **toward the ceiling to pass**. Return upright between tilts. Large touch buttons are always available, and there's an optional touch-only mode.

- Practice both tilts before starting.
- 30, 60, 90 or 120-second rounds with a three-second ready countdown.
- Sound cues, vibration, gentle-tilt option and large self-fitting card text.
- Pause/resume; leaving the app automatically pauses the clock.
- Review every answer and tap to correct accidental scores.
- Last 20 rounds saved locally.
- Previously displayed cards are excluded until the deck is exhausted, including after restarting the app.
- Day, night and system themes.

## Checked before release

95 unit tests and 12 Android instrumented tests passed, including 108 long-card/layout cases. The signed release APK was installed on an Android 14 emulator and tested through actual simulated tilt events, touch gameplay, timer expiry, pause, review, themes and restart persistence. Signature verification passed; no app crashes appeared in the crash log.

Physical-phone tilt comfort, speaker volume and vibration strength still need your hands-on playtest. The emulator does not prove how every phone feels.
