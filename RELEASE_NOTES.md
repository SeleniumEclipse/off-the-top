# Off the Top 1.5.0 — Three more decks

- **Characters:** 580 fictional character names.
- **Silent Acting:** 534 prompts. Clue givers act without speaking; the guesser can answer aloud.
- **Food & Drink:** 550 foods, ingredients and drinks.
- **Six-deck picker:** swipe or use the arrows between full-size card stacks, with original category icons. Narrower screens show two cards per page.
- **Shared played-card memory:** switching decks avoids repeating an overlapping title you've already seen. Existing settings, scores and played-card history are preserved on update.
- Same green, cream and red Card Table theme. No gradients, emoji, card suits or outer table outline.

The original three decks are unchanged. There are now **3,508 cards across decks, representing 2,933 unique titles**. This adds **1,089 new titles**; the other 575 entries deliberately reuse suitable existing prompts in the specialized decks. [Deck counts and reuse](https://github.com/SeleniumEclipse/off-the-top/blob/v1.5.0/DECKS.md).

**[Download the Android APK](https://github.com/SeleniumEclipse/off-the-top/releases/download/v1.5.0/Off-the-Top-1.5.0.apk)** and install over the existing app. No uninstall needed. Android 8.0+; offline, with no microphone permission.

**140 unit tests and 38 Android tests passed.** The signed APK was checked for six-deck play, paging, readable labels, saved rounds, tilt controls and real-time expiry on the emulator. Physical-phone tilt comfort, sound and vibration still need hands-on testing. [Validation details](https://github.com/SeleniumEclipse/off-the-top/blob/v1.5.0/VALIDATION.md).

Characters includes names from mature works; Food & Drink includes alcoholic drinks. Familiarity varies by group—pass unknown or unwanted prompts. Independently curated lists and original category artwork; not affiliated with Heads Up! or the referenced franchises.
